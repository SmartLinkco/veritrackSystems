VeriTrack Global Business Awards

Measured excellence. Celebrated annually.

VGBA recognises client organisations that lead in workforce attendance—not on opinion, but on live performance data captured throughout the year.

Performance. Presence. Progress.

How it works

1

Measure

Track attendance rate, punctuality, checkout completion, and sync reliability all year.

2

Rank

A Performance Score (0–100) identifies top performers by sector and size band.

3

Recognise

Winners receive portal badges, press releases, and optional case features.

4

Improve

Every client gets benchmark insights—the programme drives improvement, not just ceremony.

Performance Score methodology

Rankings use the same composite score (0–100) visible in your VeriTrack dashboards. Weights applied each year (subject to annual review):

  • Attendance rate — 50%
  • Punctuality — 30%
  • Checkout completion — 15%
  • Online sync reliability — 5%
Performance dashboard — live metrics that feed VGBA

Award categories

Enterprise Excellence

Top Performance Score among enterprise portal clients.

Education & Campus Leadership

Top among college portals, including class attendance where enabled.

Punctuality Award

Lowest sustained late-rate among qualifying clients.

Operational Integrity

Best checkout completion and online sync reliability.

Trust safeguards

  • Active subscription and minimum data window required (e.g. 9 months of eligible logs).
  • Clients may opt out of public ranking while remaining in private benchmarks.
  • VeriTrack staff and demo tenants are excluded.
