# Remote Work Policy

**VeriTrack Systems** · Effective: 11 June 2026  
**Applies to:** All remote and hybrid personnel  
**Owner:** Chief Operating Officer · **Contact:** info@veritrack.cloud

---

## 1. Policy statement

VeriTrack Systems operates as a **remote-first SaaS organisation**. Team members may work from home, co-working spaces, or other suitable locations in Ghana and approved regions, provided they maintain security, availability, and quality standards for client deployments and support.

Registered operations: **Accra, Ghana**. Remote work does not change our obligations under Ghana law, DPC registration, or client data processing agreements.

## 2. Eligibility & locations

- Remote work is the **default mode** for engineering, product, support, marketing, and commercial roles unless a role explicitly requires on-site client activity
- Work may be performed from **Ghana** or other countries **with prior written approval** from leadership (tax, labour, and data-transfer implications must be considered)
- Personnel must disclose their **primary work country and time zone** and notify operations of extended travel (>14 days) that may affect availability or data residency

**Not permitted** without approval: working from high-risk public networks for production access; using sanctioned jurisdictions for core infrastructure administration.

## 3. Working hours & availability

VeriTrack does not mandate a single 9–5 desk presence. We optimise for **outcomes and coverage**:

| Expectation | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Core collaboration window** | 10:00–15:00 **GMT** overlap for meetings, deployments, and urgent support |
| **Response time** | Email/chat within **one business day**; critical incidents per on-call roster |
| **Client-facing roles** | Maintain published support hours; use Calendly links for external bookings |
| **Meetings** | Video on when practical; agendas and notes shared in writing |

Overtime or weekend work for releases should be **planned and compensated** per employment contract. Sustained unsustainable hours should be raised with your manager.

## 4. Communication & collaboration

- **Primary channels:** Company Google Workspace (email, Drive, Meet), approved chat (e.g. Google Chat or designated Slack), and project tracking as assigned
- **Documentation first:** Decisions, client configurations, and incident timelines must be written down—not only verbal
- **Status updates:** Remote staff post brief daily or weekly status in the team channel (format defined by team lead)
- **Out of office:** Calendar blocks and handover notes required for absences >1 day

## 5. Work environment & ergonomics

You are responsible for a **safe, professional workspace**:

- Reliable internet (minimum **10 Mbps** recommended for video and deployments)
- Backup connectivity (mobile hotspot) for critical on-call shifts
- Quiet space for client calls; professional background or blur when on video
- Basic ergonomics (chair, screen height) to prevent injury

VeriTrack may provide a **one-time or annual remote equipment allowance** as stated in your contract. Company-owned devices remain VeriTrack property and must be returned on exit.

## 6. Information security (remote-specific)

Remote workers must:

- Enable **full-disk encryption** and screen lock on devices accessing VeriTrack systems
- Keep OS and browsers **patched**; use company-managed accounts where provided
- Use **MFA** on Google Workspace, repository, and cloud console accounts
- Avoid public Wi‑Fi for Super Admin or production tasks without **VPN** or secure hotspot
- Not allow family or housemates to use devices logged into VeriTrack accounts
- **Clear desk** for written notes containing client names, credentials, or personal data

See [Acceptable Use Policy](./ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md) for full system rules.

## 7. Confidentiality & privacy at home

- Client attendance photos, GPS logs, and HR exports must not be visible to others in your household
- Use privacy filters on laptops in shared spaces when handling dashboards
- Shred or securely delete printed client material

## 8. Client visits & on-site work

Some roles may require **on-site deployment, training, or sales** at client premises in Ghana:

- Travel is arranged and approved in advance
- On-site personnel represent VeriTrack professionally and follow client site rules
- Expenses reimbursed per company expense policy and receipt requirements

## 9. Health, wellbeing & leave

- Take **annual leave** as contracted; ensure handover for active client tickets
- Report illness promptly; do not perform production changes while impaired
- Mental health support: speak with leadership about workload adjustments

## 10. Performance management

Remote employment is evaluated on:

- Delivery of portals, fixes, and client outcomes
- Responsiveness and communication quality
- Security and policy compliance
- Collaboration across time zones

Performance issues are addressed through the same procedures as office-based roles.

## 11. Policy violations

Failure to maintain security, availability, or confidentiality while remote may result in revocation of remote privileges, mandatory return to supervised work arrangements, or termination.

## 12. Review

This policy is reviewed **annually** or when material changes occur (new regions, new tooling, regulatory updates).
