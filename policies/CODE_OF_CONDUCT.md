# Code of Conduct

**VeriTrack Systems** · Effective: 11 June 2026  
**Applies to:** Employees, contractors, interns, and representatives acting on behalf of VeriTrack  
**Owner:** Chief Executive Officer · **Contact:** info@veritrack.cloud

---

## 1. Our standard

VeriTrack sells **accountability at scale**—verified attendance, live operational truth, and trust across pharmacies, schools, logistics firms, and multi-branch enterprises. Our conduct must match that promise. This Code applies in all professional settings: client calls, partner meetings, support tickets, social media, conferences, and internal collaboration—**especially in a remote-first team** where written tone and responsiveness shape culture.

## 2. Core values

| Value | In practice |
|-------|-------------|
| **Integrity** | We do not falsify data, metrics, demos, or client outcomes |
| **Accountability** | We own mistakes, fix them quickly, and communicate clearly |
| **Respect** | We treat clients, end users, partners, and colleagues with dignity |
| **Confidentiality** | We protect client and company information |
| **Excellence** | We ship reliable portals and dashboards; we do not cut corners on security |

## 3. Professional behaviour

### 3.1 With clients & end users

- Respond within agreed **support windows**; set clear expectations when delayed
- Speak plainly—avoid jargon when onboarding HR teams and operators
- **Never blame** end users for platform confusion; escalate product issues internally
- Support administrators with patience; remember many users are non-technical
- Do not discuss one client’s operations, pricing, or performance with another client
- Escalate billing disputes, legal threats, or regulatory complaints to leadership promptly

### 3.2 With partners & referral agents

- Treat partners as independent businesses governed by [Partner Terms](../agentsTCs.html)
- Do not promise commission rates, tiers, or marketing support outside published terms
- Investigate suspected partner misconduct (misrepresentation, data misuse) and report to operations

### 3.3 With each other (remote team)

- Default to **transparent, documented communication** (written summaries after calls)
- Respect time zones—Ghana (GMT), and colleagues in other regions (e.g. UAE, West Africa)
- Be present and engaged in video meetings; cameras encouraged but not mandatory
- Give direct feedback privately; celebrate wins publicly
- **No harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, or retaliation**

## 4. Anti-harassment & equal opportunity

VeriTrack provides equal opportunity regardless of gender, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, sexual orientation, or nationality. We do not tolerate:

- Offensive jokes, slurs, or imagery in any work channel
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Threats, shouting, or deliberate humiliation in meetings
- Exclusion based on protected characteristics

Report violations to the CEO or info@veritrack.cloud (subject: CONDUCT). Reports are taken seriously; retaliation against reporters is grounds for termination.

## 5. Conflicts of interest

Disclose promptly if you:

- Have a financial interest in a competitor, partner, or prospective client
- Employ or contract with a client’s staff or leadership outside VeriTrack work
- Accept gifts or payments from vendors or clients beyond nominal courtesy (e.g. branded merchandise, modest meals)
- Use non-public VeriTrack or client information for personal gain

Leadership will decide on recusal, disclosure to affected parties, or restriction of duties.

## 6. Confidentiality & insider information

You may access non-public information about:

- Product roadmap, pricing, and security incidents
- Client lists, tenant configurations, and attendance performance (including VGBA rankings before publication)
- Unannounced partnerships or commercial negotiations

**Do not disclose** this information outside VeriTrack except as required for your job with appropriate safeguards. NDAs with clients and partners remain in force after employment ends.

## 7. Accurate representation

When representing VeriTrack publicly or in sales:

- Do not inflate client counts, check-in volumes, or uptime unless verified from the operations registry
- Distinguish **live production** features from roadmap or optional add-ons
- Present VGBA as a **ceremonial recognition programme** derived from operational metrics—not a government or ISO certification
- State clearly that VeriTrack is **DPC-certified** as a data controller/processor under Ghana law; do not overstate certification scope

## 8. Use of company brand

- Use official logos and colours only for approved materials
- Personal social media must clarify that views are your own unless posting as an authorised company spokesperson
- Do not imply endorsement by clients without written permission

## 9. Compliance with law

Personnel must comply with applicable laws, including:

- Ghana **Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)** and DPC guidance
- Client-country laws where you knowingly process data (e.g. POPIA, NDPR, Kenya DPA) as directed by leadership and contracts
- Anti-bribery and anti-corruption principles in commercial dealings

Seek guidance before activities that may trigger licensing, export control, or education-sector regulations.

## 10. Consequences

Breaches may result in coaching, formal warning, loss of client access, termination, or legal action depending on severity. Criminal conduct (fraud, unauthorised access, identity theft) will be reported to authorities.

## 11. Acknowledgement

All personnel confirm they have read and will follow this Code at onboarding and upon material updates.
