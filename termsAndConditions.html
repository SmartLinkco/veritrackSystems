2. Scope of services

VeriTrack provides a multi-tenant workforce attendance and analytics platform. Depending on your sector and configuration, services may include:

  • Enterprise workforce portals — Photo-verified check-in/out, staff ID or QR flows, GPS geofencing, optional offline queue and sync, branch-level visibility.
  • College mode — Class and course attendance, lecturer assignments, and campus analytics.
  • Logistics and field operations — Mobile-first check-in for distributed teams and geofenced sites.
  • Event and visitor attendance — Registration and on-site check-in for events (quoted separately where applicable).
  • Student attendance systems — Dedicated student register and scanner flows where deployed.
  • Reporting and optional integrations — Payroll-ready exports by default; direct payroll connectors and advanced modules available as add-ons.

Feature availability depends on your subscription tier, portal type, and written configuration. VeriTrack may improve, modify, or retire features with reasonable notice where changes materially affect paid functionality.
